Position 1: Business Analyst

Reference Number: BA001

Position Title: Business Analyst

Brief Description: We are seeking a skilled Business Analyst to join our team. The Business Analyst will be responsible for analyzing business processes, identifying areas for improvement, and implementing solutions to enhance efficiency and effectiveness.

Salary Range: $60,000 - $80,000 per year

Reports to: Project Manager

Key Responsibilities:

  • Analyze business processes and systems
  • Identify areas for improvement and recommend solutions
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements
  • Develop and maintain project documentation
  • Conduct cost-benefit analysis
  • Monitor project progress and ensure timely delivery
  • Provide training and support to end-users

Required Qualifications:

Essential

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, or related field
  • Proven experience as a Business Analyst
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities

Preferable

  • Master's degree in Business Administration or related field
  • Experience with project management methodologies
  • Certification in Business Analysis (e.g., CBAP)

Position 2: Web Developer

Reference Number: WD002

Position Title: Web Developer

Brief Description: We are seeking a skilled web developer to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a strong passion for web development, excellent problem-solving skills, and a keen eye for detail.

Salary Range: $50,000 - $70,000 per year

Reports to: Senior Web Developer

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain websites and web applications
  • Collaborate with designers and other team members to implement website features and functionality
  • Optimize websites for speed, performance, and responsiveness
  • Debug and troubleshoot issues with websites and web applications
  • Implement security measures to protect websites and user data
  • Stay updated on emerging web development technologies and best practices
  • Provide technical support and assistance to clients and stakeholders

Required Qualifications

Essential

  • Proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Experience with front-end frameworks such as React, Vue.js, or Angular
  • Strong understanding of responsive web design principles
  • Experience with version control systems such as Git
  • Excellent problem-solving and debugging skills

Preferable

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
  • Experience with back-end development using Node.js, Python, or PHP
  • Knowledge of database systems such as MySQL, MongoDB, or PostgreSQL
